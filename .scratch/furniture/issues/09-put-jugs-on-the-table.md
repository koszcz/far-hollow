# 09: Put jugs on the table

**What to build:** The player can put jugs on a table, where they stand and keep their water. A table then becomes a place to keep drinks, not just a place to eat. This builds on the table (ticket 04) and the jug from the clay feature.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling. Might reuse `table`, `jug`, and `jug-icons`.

Questions for grilling:

- How many jugs fit on one table?
- Can other things go on a table too, such as food or a pot, or only jugs?
- Can the player drink straight from a jug on the table, or must they pick it up first?
- Does putting a jug on the table free its weight from the backpack?
