# 01: Decorate the cabin, with animal skins and other pieces

**What to build:** The player can make decorations and place them in and around the cabin, such as animal skins on the floor or walls. Decorations make the cabin feel like the player's own. This builds on the hunting feature and on furniture.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling. Might reuse the deer from the hunting feature.

Questions for grilling:

- Which decorations come first, and what does each one cost?
- Where does an animal skin come from: butchering a deer, or a new step such as tanning?
- Do decorations do anything, such as add warmth or count toward a snug room, or are they only for looks?
- Can the player place decorations on walls, or only on floors and furniture?
- What does "more artistic" mean for the look of the game's current pieces?
