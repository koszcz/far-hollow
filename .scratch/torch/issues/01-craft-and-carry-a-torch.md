## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Add the torch as a hand recipe that the player lights with the steel and flint and carries for 3 game hours of light.

**Current behavior:**

- The game has no torch. The only light at night comes from lit campfires and fireplaces, which the player cannot move.
- The glossary entry for the torch is a one-line stub that mentions mounting. Mounting is listed under "Later" in the spec.
- Campfire lights stay in the scene at zero strength while unlit, so lighting a fire does not make three.js rebuild every material.

**Desired behavior:**

- The craft menu offers the torch as a hand recipe: 1 stick and 1 bark, with no tool.
- A torch is a new `BackpackContent` that weighs 0.8 kg. It shows in the hotbar with its own icon.
- Unlit torches behave like other backpack contents. The player can drop them, they form piles, and they go in a chest or on a shelf.
- "Light" is the torch's main action in the backpack. It moves one torch from the backpack to the player's hand, lit, with 3 game hours of burning time.
- "Light" shows greyed with a reason when the player has no steel and flint, or already holds a lit torch (ADR 0008).
- The lit torch lights the ground within about 6 m of the player. It casts no shadows.
- The lit torch's burning time runs down with game time. At zero the torch is gone.
- The hotbar shows the lit torch with a gauge of the burning time left.
- The player keeps the lit torch while walking, working, carrying a log on the shoulder, and dragging a sled.
- "Put out" throws the lit torch away. A torch cannot be lit twice.
- Sleeping on a bedroll or in a bed puts out the lit torch, as "Put out" does.
- A torch gives no warmth. Rain does not put it out.
- The save holds unlit torches and the lit torch with its burning time left. The save version goes up, and an upgrade step loads older saves.

**Key interfaces:**

- `BackpackContent`: gains the torch.
- The hand recipe list: gains the torch recipe.
- Player state: gains an optional lit torch with its burning time left. It is not a backpack content, so it does not use a backpack slot.
- `Command`: new commands to light and put out a torch. Each one refuses with a `Refusal` that the UI can show.
- `GameView`: exposes the lit torch and its burning time left, so the hotbar and the renderer can read it.
- The sleep commands (`sleep`, `sleepInBed`): put out the lit torch when sleep starts.
- `SAVE_UPGRADES`: a new upgrade step for the torch fields.
- The renderer: the hand torch's light is a `PointLight` that is always in the scene. It sits at zero strength when there is no torch, as the campfire glow does. Ticket 02 adds planted torches and limits real lights to 4, so build the light so it can join a small fixed set of lights.
- The asset catalogue: draft entries `torch` and `torch-icons` built in code (ADR 0006). The flame reuses `fire-effect` at a small size.

**Out of scope:**

- Planting a torch and the 4-light limit. Ticket 02 adds them.
- A torch mounted on a log wall.
- Lighting a torch from a fire, or lighting a fire with a torch.
- Warmth from a torch, or torches that scare animals.
- Darkness that slows the player or stops work.
- Changing the approved `backpack-icons` or `crafting-icons` assets.
