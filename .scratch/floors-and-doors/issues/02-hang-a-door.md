# 02: Hang a door in a door frame

**What to build:** The player can hang a plank door in a door frame. The door opens by itself as the player walks through, and closes behind them.

**Blocked by:** None

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Assets:** Reuse `door-frame`. Add the drafts `door`, `door-animation`, and `floor-and-door-icons` (door icon).

- [ ] The build menu lists the door with its cost of 3 planks, and shows its icon.
- [ ] The blueprint snaps to a finished door frame that has no door. A click places it. Esc cancels.
- [ ] Planks reach the blueprint from the backpack, a parked sled next to it, and plank piles within 2 m, over several trips.
- [ ] The player holds the build action for 6 seconds to build it. Building needs no tool.
- [ ] The door shows its build stages.
- [ ] A door opens when the player comes within 1 m of the middle of its door frame, and closes once they are more than 1 m away.
- [ ] A door does not change where the player can walk, or the paths they take.
- [ ] The player can take down a door or its blueprint and get back every plank.
- [ ] The player cannot take down a door frame that has a door. The action shows greyed with the reason.
- [ ] Doors and their blueprints are saved. Whether a door stands open is not saved. The save version goes up, and older saves are upgraded when loaded.
- [ ] In the playground, a door spends no planks.
- [ ] `GLOSSARY.md` defines the door as the spec does.
- [ ] All player-facing text is in the strings file.
- [ ] Every asset under **Assets** is in the asset catalogue, reused or added as a draft (ADR 0006).
- [ ] Unit tests cover placement, plank delivery, building, opening and closing, and taking down a door and its frame.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Add the door, 3 planks that hang in a finished door frame. It swings open as the player comes near and shuts behind them.

**Current behavior:**

- A door frame is an edge piece on the edge of a foundation square. The player walks through it freely.
- An edge piece cannot come down while a roof frame rests on it. The game refuses with the "holds up a piece" notice (ADR 0008).
- Plank furniture gets planks from the backpack, a sled parked next to it, and plank piles within 2 m, over several trips.

**Desired behavior:**

- The build menu lists the door: 3 planks, 6 seconds to build, no tool. It has its own icon.
- The door blueprint snaps to a finished door frame with no door and no door blueprint. It takes the frame's edge and facing. A click places it. Esc cancels.
- Planks reach a door blueprint the same way they reach plank furniture. Reuse that path.
- A finished door opens while the player is within 1 m of the middle of its frame. It shuts once they are farther away. Whether it is open is not saved. After loading, every door starts shut.
- Doors, open or shut, never change the walk map or the paths the player takes.
- Taking down a door or its blueprint gives back every plank in it.
- A door frame with a door or door blueprint cannot come down. The take-down shows greyed with the existing "holds up a piece" reason.
- Doors and door blueprints are saved. The save version goes up with an upgrade step for older saves.

**Key interfaces:**

- `StructureKind`: gains `"door"`. A door is not an edge piece. It hangs in one, so the edge stays held by the frame.
- `StructureDefinition` for the door: cost of 3 planks, `buildSeconds` 6, a new `FinishedMarker` for doors. Its `snap` finds the nearest finished door frame.
- The open or shut state is worked out from the player's position. It may live in the sim view or in the renderer, but not in the save.
- `holdsUpAnother` for a door frame: must count a door or door blueprint in it.
- Save schema and `SAVE_UPGRADES`: one new upgrade for doors. If ticket 01 lands first, add a separate upgrade after its one.
- `asset-manifest.json`: draft entries `door` and `door-animation`. Add the door icon to `floor-and-door-icons`, creating the entry if ticket 01 has not.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Every checkbox in this ticket's list above is met.
- [ ] A unit test refuses a door blueprint on a door frame blueprint, on a log wall, and on a frame that already has a door.
- [ ] A unit test opens a door when the player is 0.9 m from the frame's middle, and shuts it at 1.1 m.
- [ ] A unit test shows the walk map is the same with and without a door.
- [ ] A save from the previous version loads with no doors.
- [ ] `make check` passes.

**Out of scope:**

- Snug rooms or any change to warmth. That is ticket 03.
- Doors the player bars, locks, or leaves open.
- Animals and doors.
- Approving the draft assets. The owner does that with `make-asset`.
