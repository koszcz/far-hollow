# 07: Timed tasks walk to their thing through the approach module

**What to build:** Every task that ticket 01 leaves behind walks the player within reach through the approach module that ticket 01 adds. Most of these tasks keep working after the player arrives, such as chopping, debarking, crafting at a station, taking down, gathering stone, fishing, and building. Drinking and filling a pot also move here. Each activity module keeps only the state of the work itself.

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Assets:** None. This ticket changes no asset.

## Why

Ticket 01 moves the act-once tasks onto the approach module. The owner split the timed tasks into this ticket during triage on 2026-11-21, because one ticket for all 22 task components would make a very long session. Until this ticket lands, the timed activity modules still walk the player themselves.

## Decisions already made

These were settled in triage for ticket 01. Read ADR 0010, ADR 0011, the glossary entry **Approach**, and ticket 01's agent brief first.

- The approach stores the command that started it. On arrival, the game checks the command's refusal again, then carries out the command's act step. It does not send the command again.
- The act step starts the timed work. The work keeps its own component, such as `chopTask` with `secondsDone`. That component comes to mean "working" only, keeps its name, and its comment says so.
- Older saves load and play on (ADR 0011). A save upgrade turns a timed task saved before its work began into an approach that holds the matching command: the player still has a `walkPath` and `secondsDone` is 0. A timed task already under way stays as it is. The upgrade writes command shapes as literals.

## What to do

The agent brief under Comments is the contract. It corrects the task list and the save upgrade rule above, after triage on 2026-11-21 checked them against the code.

1. Move each activity module onto the approach, one module per commit.
2. Remove each module's own walk and its reach check on arrival. Keep only the work state in its task component.
3. Add the save upgrade described in the brief, as one new entry at the end of `SAVE_UPGRADES`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Outside the approach module and the walking module, only the free walk command calls `walkTo`, `walkWithin`, `walkBeside`, `walkOntoOrWithinReach`, or the building module's `walkWithinReach`.
- [ ] Each timed task component holds only the state of the work.
- [ ] A test loads a save made at the previous version with a `chopTask` mid-walk. The player arrives and chops. A second test loads a save with a `chopTask` mid-chop. The chop continues from its saved progress.
- [ ] Existing behaviour tests pass, such as the chopping, crafting, fishing, and action queue tests.
- [ ] `make check` passes.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Move every task that ticket 01 leaves behind onto the approach module. The timed tasks keep a task component that holds only the state of the work.

**Current behavior:**

- Ticket 01 adds the approach module and moves the act-once tasks onto it. Read ADR 0010, ADR 0011, the glossary entry **Approach**, and ticket 01's agent brief first.
- The timed activity modules still walk the player themselves. Each sets its task component when the command arrives, starts a walk, and on each step skips the player while they have a `walkPath`. Once the walk ends, it checks reach and then does the work.
- These task components still exist: `chopTask`, `debarkTask`, `craftTask`, `takeDownTask`, `stoneTask`, `fishTask`, `buildTask`, `drinkTask`, and `fillPotTask`. Ticket 01 may leave one or two more, with a reason in its commit message. Check its commits and move those too.
- Each of these task components mixes "walking to the thing" with "working on it".
- Some tasks do not fit the timed pattern:
  - `drinkTask` and `fillPotTask` only mark a walk. On arrival the player drinks, or reaches for the water, in one step. Ticket 01's brief listed them as timed by mistake.
  - `buildTask` has no `secondsDone`. The build progress lives on the blueprint.
  - Fishing casts when the command arrives, before the walk. The cast adds 1 to `fishCasts` and picks a seeded bite time from that count.
  - Crafting by hand does not walk. Only crafting at a station walks.
  - Splitting does not walk. `splitTask` has no target.

**Desired behavior:**

- Each command that walks first starts an approach. The approach gives the command's target, reach, and walk style, as ticket 01 set up. Its act step starts the work on arrival.
- Each timed task component starts on arrival and means "working" only. It keeps its name. Its comment says it holds the work in progress.
- The module's own check for being out of reach on arrival goes away. The approach owns it. Checks that can change while the work goes on stay with the work. Examples are the tree still standing, the drawknife still in the backpack, and the logs still lying beside the station.
- Drinking and filling a pot become act-once tasks. Delete `drinkTask` and `fillPotTask`, together with their save schema entries and task parts. Filling the pot calls `HandReach.start` in its act step.
- Fishing casts on arrival, in its act step. A fishing walk that is cancelled no longer counts a cast.
- Crafting at a station walks through the approach. Crafting by hand stays as it is.
- Splitting does not change.
- Older saves load and play on (ADR 0011, a hard rule). One new save upgrade does this:
  - A player with a timed task component and a `walkPath` is still walking. The upgrade replaces the task component with an approach that holds the matching command. This rule covers every task, including `buildTask`, which has no `secondsDone`.
  - A timed task component without a `walkPath` is work in progress. The upgrade keeps it as it is.
  - A `drinkTask` or `fillPotTask` always becomes an approach that holds the matching command.
  - A `fishTask` saved mid-walk also takes 1 off `fishCasts`. The cast on arrival then uses the same seed as before.
  - The upgrade writes command shapes as literals. It never calls the game's current code.

**Key interfaces:**

- The approach module and the player's pending approach component from ticket 01. Do not change how they work. Add walk styles only if a module needs one the approach does not offer yet, such as walking to the nearest walkable spot by the river.
- The command rules in `commandRules`: each timed rule that walks first gives the approach its target, reach, walk style, and act step.
- The timed task components on the player entity, in the save schema, and in the task parts used by `playerIsBusy`.
- `SAVE_UPGRADES`: one new upgrade at the end of the list.
