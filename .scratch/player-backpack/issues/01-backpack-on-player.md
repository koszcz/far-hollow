# 01: Show the backpack on the player

**Status:** resolved

**Assets:** changes the approved `player`. The owner approved this change in chat on 2026-11-12. Adds the draft `backpack`.

**What to build:** The game talks about the player's backpack, in the HUD and in the glossary, but the player has no backpack on their back. The owner reported this in chat on 2026-11-12 and asked for a backpack on the player.

- [x] The player wears a canvas backpack on their back, with a leather flap, a front pocket, and shoulder straps.
- [x] The backpack moves with the chest in every pose: idle, walking, chopping, splitting, carrying a log, and building.
- [x] The axe still hangs on the back while the hands are busy. It hangs on the outside of the backpack, not inside it.
- [x] The log on the shoulder does not pass through the backpack.
- [x] The backpack is listed in `asset-manifest.json` as the draft `backpack`, and the asset showcase shows it at `/?asset=backpack`.
