# 04: Tip a sled into a woodpile, and take a sled down

**What to build:** A sled parked next to a woodpile tips its logs onto the woodpile. The player can take a sled down for its materials.

**Blocked by:** 02 (Load and unload a parked sled)

**Status:** resolved

**Assets:** Reuse `sled` and `woodpile`.

- [ ] Tipping out next to a woodpile puts the logs on the woodpile.
- [ ] Logs that do not fit on the woodpile are laid on the ground beside the sled.
- [ ] The player can take down a parked sled and get back 2 logs and 4 sticks.
- [ ] Any logs on the sled are laid on the ground beside it first.
- [ ] Unit tests cover a woodpile with room, a nearly full woodpile, and taking down a loaded sled.
