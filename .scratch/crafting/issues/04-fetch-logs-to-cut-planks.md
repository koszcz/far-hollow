# 04: Fetch logs to cut planks

**What to build:** When the player cuts planks at the sawhorse, they walk to nearby logs and pick them up on their own, the way construction fetches materials. Today the player must carry each log to the sawhorse by hand. This builds on cutting planks (ticket 03) and the fetch rule from the fetch-materials feature.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling. Might reuse `logs`, `debarked-log`, `sawhorse`, and `carry-in-hands-animation`.

Questions for grilling:

- How far does the player look for logs: the same 10 m as construction fetch, or another distance?
- Does the player fetch from woodpiles and parked sleds too?
- Does one order cut one plank, or does the player keep fetching until the logs run out?
- Should other station recipes fetch their materials the same way?
