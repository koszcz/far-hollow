# 01: Food spoils, and the player preserves it

**What to build:** Fresh food goes bad after a while, so the player cannot stockpile raw fish for weeks. The player can preserve food to keep it for longer, for example by smoking fish, drying berries and mushrooms, or storing food in clay crocks. The clay spec left crocks and spoilage out on purpose. With seasons (`.scratch/seasons/`), this makes preparing for winter a real task.

**Blocked by:** None, but grill it after `.scratch/seasons/issues/01-seasons-and-a-first-winter.md` so the two fit together.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling. A smoking or drying rack and a crock are likely draft assets.

Questions for grilling:

- Which foods spoil: raw fish, cooked fish, berries, mushrooms, garden produce, meat?
- How long does each food keep, in days?
- Does food spoil all at once, or does it go stale first and fill less hunger?
- What happens to spoiled food: does it disappear, or can the player still eat it and risk sickness?
- Which ways to preserve food are in: smoking, drying, salting, crocks, or a cold cellar?
- Is a smoking or drying rack furniture built from the build menu, or a work station?
- Does smoking need a lit fire under or next to the rack?
- Does preserved food fill as much hunger as fresh food?
- Does food in a chest, on a shelf, or in a crock keep longer than food in the backpack?
- Does cold weather in winter slow spoilage?
- How does the player see how fresh a food is?
- What do older saves do with the food they already hold?
