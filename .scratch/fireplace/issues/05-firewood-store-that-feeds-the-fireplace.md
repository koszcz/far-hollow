# 05: A firewood store by the chimney that feeds the fireplace

**What to build:** The player can build a firewood store next to the fireplace and chimney. The fireplace takes firewood from it on its own, so the fire keeps burning without the player feeding it by hand. This builds on the fire in a fireplace (ticket 03), the chimney, and the woodpile from the storage feature.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling. Might reuse `woodpile`, `firewood`, `fireplace`, and `chimney`.

Questions for grilling:

- Is this a new structure, or a woodpile placed within a set distance of the fireplace?
- How much firewood does it hold, and what does it cost to build?
- When does the fireplace take firewood: when the fire runs low, or on a timer?
- Does it relight a fire the player has put out, or only feed a burning fire?
- Does the same store work for a campfire?
