# 01: A chimney through the roof

**What to build:** The player can build a stone chimney that rises from a fireplace up through the roof. Smoke from a lit fireplace then rises out of the chimney top, above the cabin. Today the fireplace has only a short chimney that stops below the roof. The fireplace spec left a chimney through the roof out on purpose.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** Reuses `fireplace` and `fire-effect`. A tall chimney, and a roof section with a hole for it, are likely draft assets. Decide during grilling.

Questions for grilling:

- Is the chimney a separate building piece on top of a fireplace, or a later build stage of the fireplace itself?
- How much stone does it take, and how long does it take to build?
- Can the player build it before the roof, after the roof, or both?
- How does a roof section fit round the chimney? Does it need a gap, and does the gap still keep the rain off?
- How tall is it? Does its height depend on the roof, such as a lean-to roof or a ridge roof?
- Does a chimney change anything in play, such as a snug room, warmth, or fuel? Or is it only for looks?
- Does a fireplace with no chimney cause a problem indoors, such as smoke in the room?
- Where can a fireplace with a chimney stand: against a wall only, or anywhere in a room?
- How does a chimney work with a second storey (`.scratch/second-storey/`)?
- Can the player take down a chimney, and do they get back every stone?
- How do older saves treat a fireplace already under a roof?
