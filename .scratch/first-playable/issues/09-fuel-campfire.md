# 09: Fuel the campfire

**What to build:** The player lights the campfire with the fire striker and adds sticks or firewood as fuel. The fire burns its fuel down and goes out when the fuel runs out. A lit fire lights up the area around it at night.

**Blocked by:** 06 (Day and night clock with a debug overlay), 07 (Gather fuel), 08 (Build a campfire)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The player can light a campfire that has fuel, using the fire striker.
- [ ] The player can add sticks or firewood from the backpack as fuel.
- [ ] Firewood burns longer than sticks.
- [ ] The fire burns fuel over game time. It goes out when the fuel runs out.
- [ ] A lit campfire lights a visible area around it at night.
- [ ] The player can see roughly how much fuel is left.
- [ ] Unit tests cover lighting, fuel burn rates, and the fire going out.
