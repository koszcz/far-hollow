# 01: The journal suggests goals

**What to build:** A journal suggests goals to a new player, such as a first fire, a first shelter, and a first cabin. Following it is optional. The game never ends. The journal is milestone 10 in the design spec. Friends of the owner are now interested in playing, so a new player needs a way to learn the game.

**Status:** needs-triage

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during triage.

Questions for triage:

- Which goals does the journal list, and in what order?
- Does each goal explain how to do it, such as which key opens the build menu?
- Does the journal also teach the controls, or is that a separate first-time help?
- How does the player open the journal, and does it show a notice when a goal is done?
- Can the player turn the journal off?
- Does the journal mark goals done that the player finished before it existed, in older saves?
- Is each goal done for good, or can it be undone, such as when the player takes down their first cabin?
- Are the goals saved?
