# 01: Build a bridge across the river

**What to build:** The player can build a bridge across the river, so they can cross it in any season. It uses logs and planks like other building pieces.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling. Might reuse `logs`, `debarked-log`, and `floor`.

Questions for grilling:

- How can the player cross the river today, and what problem does a bridge solve?
- What does a bridge cost, and how many stages does its construction have?
- Can the player build a bridge anywhere along the river, or only at narrow spots?
- Does a bridge change fishing or the fish trap near it?
