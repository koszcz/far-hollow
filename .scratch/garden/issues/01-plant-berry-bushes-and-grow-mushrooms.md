# 01: Plant berry bushes and grow mushrooms near camp

**What to build:** The player can plant berry bushes near camp and grow mushrooms on logs. Food then grows close to home instead of only in the wild. This builds on planting saplings in `.scratch/regrowth/`.

**Status:** needs-triage

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during triage. Reuse `berry-bush` and `mushrooms` if they fit.

Questions for triage:

- How does the player get a berry bush to plant: dig up a wild one like a sapling, or gather seeds?
- How long does a planted bush take to bear berries, and how often does it bear again?
- How does a mushroom log work: any log left on the ground, or a log the player prepares?
- Does a garden need watering, or does rain do it?
- Can the player plant anywhere, or only on certain ground, such as a clearing?
- Does planting change the food balance too much, so the player never needs to forage or fish?
