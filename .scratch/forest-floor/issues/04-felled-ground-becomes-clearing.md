# 04: Felled ground becomes a clearing

**What to build:** Where the player fells many trees, the forest floor slowly turns into a clearing over many days. Grass and flowers move in as needles and ferns fade.

**Blocked by:** 02 (Undergrowth)

**Status:** deferred

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during triage.

Questions for triage:

- How many trees must go, and how close together, before the ground changes?
- How many days does the change take?
- Does a regrown tree turn the ground back into forest floor?
- Ground types would then change during a game. Do they need saving, or can they be worked out from the saved stumps?
