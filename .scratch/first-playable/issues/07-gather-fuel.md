# 07: Gather fuel

**What to build:** The player can gather the materials a campfire needs. They pick up sticks from the forest floor. They split a log into firewood with the axe.

**Blocked by:** 05 (Chop a tree and carry a log)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Sticks lie on the forest floor. Their positions come from the seed.
- [ ] Left-clicking a stick walks the player to it and puts it in the backpack.
- [ ] The player can split a log into firewood with the axe. Firewood goes in the backpack.
- [ ] The backpack has a weight limit. The player cannot pick up more than it allows.
- [ ] The player can see how many sticks and how much firewood they carry.
- [ ] Unit tests cover picking up sticks, splitting logs, and the weight limit.
