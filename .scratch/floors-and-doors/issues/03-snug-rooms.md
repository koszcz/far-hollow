# 03: Snug rooms keep out the cold

**What to build:** A room with a floor on every square and a door in every door frame is snug. In a snug room, the player's warmth drains at half the usual rate at night.

**Blocked by:** 01 (Lay a floor), 02 (Hang a door)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Assets:** None.

- [ ] A room is snug when every square has a finished floor and every door frame on its edge has a finished door.

- [ ] All player-facing text is in the strings file.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** A room with a finished floor on every square and a finished door in every door frame is snug. In a snug room, the player's warmth drains at half the usual rate at night.

**Current behavior:**

- Rooms are worked out from finished pieces each time they are asked for: foundation squares closed in by log walls or door frames, with a roof over every square. They are not saved.
- A lit campfire in a room warms the whole room.
- At night, warmth drains at a fixed rate away from a lit fire. Rain adds to the drain, but not under a roof.
- Some notices, such as "survived the first night", show once as a banner.
- Tickets 01 and 02 add floors and doors.

**Desired behavior:**

- A room is snug when every square has a finished floor and every door frame on the room's edges has a finished door. Blueprints do not count. A room with no door frame is snug once every square has a floor.
- At night, a player standing in a snug room loses warmth at half the usual night rate.
- A lit campfire or fireplace in the room warms the player exactly as before.
- Snugness is worked out from the pieces, like rooms. Finishing or taking down a floor or door changes it at once. It is not saved.
- The first time the player stands in a snug room, a banner says the room now keeps out the cold. A saved flag records that it has shown. It never shows again in that game.
- The debug overlay marks which rooms are snug.

**Key interfaces:**

- The room module: add a check such as `isRoomSnug(entities, room)` beside `isRoomHeated`.
- The needs conditions passed each time slice: gain a flag such as `inSnugRoom`. The night warmth drain halves when it is set. The rain drain does not change.
- `GameNotice`: gains a notice for the snug-room banner, with its time in `NOTICE_MINUTES`, its save shape, and its text in the strings file.
- Save schema and `SAVE_UPGRADES`: one new upgrade that adds the banner flag, set to not shown.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Every checkbox in this ticket's list above is met.
- [ ] A unit test: a room with every floor and door is snug. Remove one floor and it is not. Remove one door instead and it is not.
- [ ] A unit test: a floor blueprint or door blueprint does not make a room snug.
- [ ] A unit test: one night hour drains half as much warmth in a snug room as in the same room without a door.
- [ ] A unit test: the banner shows once, and not again after a save and load.
- [ ] `make check` passes.

**Out of scope:**

- Any change to what makes a room.
- Daytime warmth, rain, or fire heat.
- Windows, barred doors, or a second storey.
