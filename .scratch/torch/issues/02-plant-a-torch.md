# 02: Plant a lit torch and pick it up again

**What to build:** The player can stand a lit torch in the ground to light a spot, and pick it up again. Only the nearest few torches cast real light, so the frame rate holds.

**Blocked by:** 01 (Craft and carry a torch)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Assets:** Reuse `torch` and `fire-effect`.

- [ ] "Plant" stands the lit torch at the player's feet, on the ground or on a foundation.
- [ ] The player cannot plant a torch within 1 m of another planted torch. The action shows greyed with the reason.
- [ ] A planted torch keeps burning for the time it had left. When it burns out, it is gone.
- [ ] Planted torches keep burning while the player sleeps. Sleep uses up their burning time, so they can burn out before the player wakes.
- [ ] A planted torch lights the ground around it and does not block walking.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Let the player plant a lit torch in the ground and pick it up again, with at most 4 torches casting real light at once.

**Current behavior:**

- After ticket 01, the player can hold one lit torch. It lights the ground around them for its burning time.
- The player cannot put a lit torch down. "Put out" and sleeping throw it away.

**Desired behavior:**

- "Plant" stands the lit torch at the player's feet, on the ground or on a foundation. The player's hand is then empty.
- "Plant" shows greyed with a reason within 1 m of another planted torch (ADR 0008).
- A planted torch is a new entity. It keeps the burning time it had left, and its time runs down with game time, sleep included. At zero it is gone.
- A planted torch shows its flame, lights the ground around it, and does not block walking.
- "Pick up" is a planted torch's main action. It puts the torch in the player's hand with its time left.
- "Pick up" shows greyed with a reason when the player already holds a lit torch.
- At most 4 torches cast real light at once: the hand torch first, then the planted torches nearest the camera. Every other planted torch shows its flame and a glow but does not light the ground.
- The save holds planted torches and their burning time left. The save version goes up, and an upgrade step loads older saves.

**Key interfaces:**

- A new planted-torch entity with a position and burning time left. It is not an obstacle on the walk map.
- `Command`: new commands to plant and pick up a torch, each with a `Refusal`.
- `ThingInReach` and the thing action menu: a planted torch with "Pick up" as its main action.
- `GameView` or `NearbyViews`: exposes planted torches so the renderer can draw them.
- The renderer: a fixed set of 4 `PointLight`s that stay in the scene. Each frame it gives them to the hand torch and the nearest planted torches, and sets the unused ones to zero strength. Torches never add or remove lights. That would make three.js rebuild every material.
- `SAVE_UPGRADES`: a new upgrade step for planted torches.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] "Plant" puts the lit torch at the player's feet on the ground and on a foundation. The hand is then empty.
- [ ] "Plant" within 1 m of a planted torch shows greyed with a reason.
- [ ] A planted torch burns for the time it had left, then is gone.
- [ ] Sleeping uses up planted torches' burning time. A torch with less time left than the sleep is gone when the player wakes.
- [ ] The player can walk through a planted torch.
- [ ] "Pick up" puts the torch in the player's hand with its time left. With a lit torch already in hand, it shows greyed with a reason.
- [ ] With 6 torches near the camera, exactly 4 cast real light: the hand torch, then the 3 nearest planted torches. The others show a flame and a glow only.
- [ ] Planting and picking up torches never changes the number of lights in the scene.
- [ ] With 20 planted torches in view, the game meets the frame-rate targets in `.scratch/first-playable/spec.md`.
- [ ] A save with planted, part-burnt torches loads back the same. A save from the version before loads without error.
- [ ] All player-facing text is in the strings file.
- [ ] Any new asset entries are in the asset catalogue as drafts (ADR 0006).
- [ ] Unit tests cover planting, the spacing rule, burning out, sleeping, picking up, and which torches cast light.
- [ ] `make check` passes.

**Out of scope:**

- A torch mounted on a log wall.
- Lighting a fire with a torch.
- Shadows from torches.
- Counting campfire and fireplace lights in the 4-light limit.
