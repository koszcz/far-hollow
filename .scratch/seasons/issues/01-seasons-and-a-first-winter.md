# 01: Seasons and a first winter

**What to build:** The valley turns through seasons, and winter is the hard one. Cold nights drain warmth faster, and wild food grows less. The player has a reason to stock firewood and food and to finish a snug cabin before the snow comes. Today the game has days but no seasons, so nothing pushes the player to prepare.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling. Snow on the ground, trees, and roofs is likely a draft asset.

Questions for grilling:

- How many seasons are there: four, or just a warm half and a cold half?
- How many days does each season last?
- Which season does a new game start in, and how long before the first winter?
- How much faster does warmth drain in winter, by day and by night?
- Does snow fall as weather, like rain? Does it lie on the ground and slow walking?
- What happens to the river in winter: does it freeze, and can the player still drink and fish?
- Do berry bushes, mushroom patches, and the garden stop growing in winter?
- Does regrowth of trees and saplings pause in winter?
- Do days get shorter in winter?
- How does the player see the season and how many days are left in it?
- Does winter end on its own, or does the valley keep cycling forever?
- How does the playground handle seasons? Can a developer jump to winter?
- What do older saves start as, and how does the save upgrade set the season?
