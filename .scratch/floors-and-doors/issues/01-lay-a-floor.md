## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Add the floor, a building piece of 2 planks that covers one finished foundation square. It changes only how the square looks.

**Current behavior:**

- A finished foundation is a bare deck. The player walks on it. Furniture, campfires, walls, door frames, and roof frames stand on it.
- A foundation cannot come down while anything stands on it, finished or as a blueprint. The game refuses with the "holds up a piece" notice (ADR 0008).
- In build mode, a click places one blueprint and leaves build mode. A left-drag pans the camera. A right-drag turns it.
- Plank furniture already gets planks from the backpack, a sled parked next to it, and plank piles within 2 m, over several trips.

**Desired behavior:**

- The build menu lists the floor: 2 planks, 4 seconds to build, no tool. It has its own icon.
- The floor blueprint snaps to the middle of a finished foundation square. It is allowed only where the square has no floor and no floor blueprint. Furniture, a campfire, or a wall or door frame on the square's edges never stop it.
- A click places one floor blueprint and keeps build mode open on the floor. Esc leaves build mode.
- Shift + left-drag places a floor blueprint on every allowed square the pointer passes over. The camera does not pan during a Shift-drag. A plain left-drag still pans. Squares that are not allowed are skipped without a refusal.
- Planks reach a floor blueprint the same way they reach plank furniture. Reuse that path. Do not add a second one.
- The floor is drawn flush on the deck. The player's standing height and the height of anything on the square do not change.
- Taking down a floor or its blueprint gives back every plank in it. Things standing on the square never stop it.
- A floor or floor blueprint counts as standing on its foundation. The foundation's take-down shows greyed with the existing "holds up a piece" reason.
- Floors and floor blueprints are saved. The save version goes up with an upgrade step for older saves.

**Key interfaces:**

- `StructureKind`: gains `"floor"`.
- `StructureDefinition` for the floor: cost of 2 planks, `buildSeconds` 4, a new `FinishedMarker` for floors. Pick the `Placement` that fits a square on a finished foundation, or add one.
- The `placeBlueprint` command: unchanged. A sweep sends one command per square.
- `holdsUpAnother` for a foundation: must count floors and their blueprints as standing on the square.
- Save schema and `SAVE_UPGRADES`: one new upgrade for floors.
- `asset-manifest.json`: draft entries `floor` and `floor-and-door-icons`. This ticket draws only the floor icon.
