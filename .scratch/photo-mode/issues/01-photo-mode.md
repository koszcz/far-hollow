# 01: Photo mode

**What to build:** The player can hide the hotbar and every panel, move the camera freely, and take a picture of their camp. The picture saves as an image file on their computer.

**Status:** deferred

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling.

Questions for grilling:

- Which key opens and closes photo mode?
- Does the game pause in photo mode?
- How free is the camera: the normal limits, or a wider angle, lower height, and wider zoom?
- Can the player change the time of day or the weather for the picture?
- What size is the picture, and does it include the game name or the seed?
- Does the player also need a way to share the seed, so friends can start in the same valley?
