# 01: A fish trap catches fish while the player works

**What to build:** The player can weave a fish trap and set it in the river. After some hours it holds a fish. The player comes back to take it. Like a snare, it rewards planning, but it needs no hunting.

**Status:** needs-triage

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during triage. Reuse `fishing-icons` for raw fish.

Questions for triage:

- What is the trap made from: sticks, bark, cord, or a mix?
- Does cord come in with this ticket, since hunting is deferred and cord was part of it?
- How long does a catch take, and how many fish does the trap hold?
- Where can the player set it: anywhere in the river, or only in shallow water?
- How close can two traps be, and how many can the player set?
- Does a trap make the fishing rod pointless?
- Is the trap saved with its time to the next catch?
