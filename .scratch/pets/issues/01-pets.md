# 01: Keep a pet

**What to build:** The player can find and keep an animal as a pet, which lives at the camp. The owner is not yet sure this fits the game.

**Status:** idea

**Assets:** None yet. Decide during grilling.

Questions for grilling:

- Does a pet fit the game at all, and what does it add?
- Which animal, and how does the player get it?
- Does a pet need food, warmth, or care, and what happens if the player neglects it?
- Does a pet help, such as warn of night or fetch sticks, or is it only company?
